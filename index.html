Decentralized Audio Asset Management for VR

Trust Layer (Ethereum Hash Registry) + Distribution Layer (WebTorrent P2P) + Client Layer (Web Audio API)

1. Trust Layer — Blockchain Asset Hashing & Registry

Upload a VR audio clip (MP3/WAV/OGG) to generate its SHA-256 fingerprint for on-chain registration.

2. Distribution Layer — P2P Swarm Chunk Distribution

Simulates multi-peer chunk routing to eliminate central server bottlenecks.
